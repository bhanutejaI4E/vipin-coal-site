How we work

Mine to plant,
one accountable partner.

A single point of contact manages your cargo end to end — from selecting the right grade to the final tonne at your gate.

  1. Sourcing

    Direct relationships with mines and producers across six origins, matched to your application and budget.

  2. Quality assurance

    Independent sampling and analysis at load and discharge ports by accredited agencies.

  3. Shipping & logistics

    Vessel chartering, port handling, customs clearance and rail or road delivery.

  4. Trade finance

    LC, documentary collection and flexible payment structures that fit your cycle.

  5. Delivery & support

    Shipment updates at every milestone and after-sales support on every contract.
