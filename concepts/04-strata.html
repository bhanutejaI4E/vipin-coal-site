FAQ

Good questions.

What quantities do you supply?

From part-cargoes of around 5,000 MT for mid-sized plants to full Panamax shipments and term contracts for utilities. Domestic stock-and-sale options are available at select ports.

Do you sell on CFR, CIF or FOB terms?

All three, plus delivered-to-plant pricing where we manage port handling and inland transport for you.

How is quality verified?

Every cargo is sampled and analysed by an accredited independent inspection agency at the load port, with discharge-port analysis available. You receive the certificate of analysis before the vessel sails.

Which Indian ports do you deliver to?

Major east and west coast terminals including Paradip, Visakhapatnam, Gangavaram, Krishnapatnam, Haldia, Mundra and Kandla — with rail or road delivery onward.

What payment terms do you offer?

Letters of credit, documentary collection (DA/DP), cash against documents and, for established customers, open-account terms.
